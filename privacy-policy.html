Lumo Gallery: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy describes how Lumo Gallery (Lumo: Private Photo Gallery, by Digiera) handles information. In short: everything stays on your phone.

What The App Does Not Do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The app does not request internet or network permission, and it contains no ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking.
  • It does not collect, send, sell or share your photos, videos, face data or any other personal data with the developer or anyone else.
  • It does not need an account.

What The App Accesses On Your Phone

  • Your photos and videos, using the media permission you grant (or only the items you choose, if you pick "selected photos"). They are read to show your gallery, to search it and to edit it.
  • Notifications, to show progress while your photos are prepared for search.
  • Your screen lock or fingerprint, only to open the Locked folder. The app never sees your fingerprint or PIN; Android checks them.

Data The App Creates, Stored Only On Your Phone

  • A search index: numbers that describe what each photo looks like, created on the phone by a built-in model. They are not copies of your photos.
  • Faces: the app does not identify or group people. When you use the face filters or retouching in the editor, it finds where faces are in that one photo, on the phone, only while you edit. Nothing about faces is stored or sent anywhere.
  • Your favorites, albums, captions, corrected dates and settings.
  • Photos and videos you move to the Locked folder. They are removed from your library and kept inside the app, encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore, which stays on your phone. Restoring them decrypts them back into your library.
  • Optionally, a backup file that you create and keep yourself. It contains your favorites, captions, albums, people and settings, not your photos.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google and the Play Store app; Lumo never sees your payment details. Lumo only asks the Play Store whether the purchase is owned, and remembers the answer on your phone.

Edits And Saved Files

Edited pictures, collages and exports are saved as new files in your own photo library. The app only changes or replaces an original photo after you confirm in Android's own dialog.

Your Control

You can rebuild the search index in Settings, delete any backup file yourself, or uninstall the app, which removes everything the app stored. Photos in your library are never removed by uninstalling.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in the app and on the store page.
